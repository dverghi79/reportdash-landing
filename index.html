The Problem

You built a 14-chart dashboard. Your client never logged in again.

01 · Manual hours

You manage 5 client accounts across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and TikTok. Every month you spend 3-4 hours per client harvesting screenshots, copying numbers, and building the same report template. That's 15-20 hours a month on admin work you don't bill for.

02 · No solo-sized tool

Tools like Reportei and SproutSocial start at $200-500/mo and assume a 5-person agency. You're solo. You don't need a white-label dashboard or 40 integrations — you need a clean PDF that makes your client feel informed and keeps them renewing at 15% month-over-month.

Your client judges your value by what they see. If your report looks like you spent 20 minutes on it, that's what they think your work is worth. If it looks like a strategic brief from a professional operation, they renew. The platforms give you the data. You just spend hours formatting it.
